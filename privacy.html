Genogram: Privacy policy

In short: Genogram collects nothing. Your genograms stay on your own device. There is no account, no server, no advertising and no tracking.

This policy covers the Genogram website (isaymotion.github.io/genogram) and the Genogram app for iPhone, iPad and Android. Effective 23 November 2026.

Information we collect

None. Genogram does not collect, receive, store, sell or share any personal information, health information, usage data, device identifiers or location. It contains no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking code, and it makes no network requests while you use it.

Where your genograms are kept

Everything you type (names, ages, conditions, relationships, notes) is saved only on the device you are using: in the browser’s storage for the website, and in the app’s own private storage on iPhone, iPad and Android. Nobody else, including the maker of the app, can see it. The website and the phone app keep separate data; you move genograms between them with a backup file.

Files you choose to save or share

A picture, a PDF, a shared genogram file or a backup file is made on your device and goes only where you choose to send it (for example Files, email or a messaging app) through your device’s own share sheet. Those files are not encrypted: treat them like any other patient document.

Face ID, Touch ID and fingerprint unlock

If you turn on the passcode lock, the phone app can open with Face ID, Touch ID or your fingerprint. This check is done entirely by your device’s operating system. Genogram only learns whether it succeeded; it never receives or stores any face or fingerprint data. The passcode itself is kept only as a salted hash on the device.

Protecting the people on the chart

Use initials or an alias, or turn on “Initials only”. Follow your hospital’s or institution’s rules on patient information. The optional passcode keeps the app closed on a shared device; it does not encrypt what is saved.

Removing your data

Delete a genogram from the home screen. To remove everything, delete the app (iPhone, iPad, Android) or clear this site’s data in your browser settings. Because nothing is kept anywhere else, deleted genograms cannot be recovered without a backup file.

Children

Genogram is a tool for health professionals and students. It is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
