## Description [4000] (same text for both stores)

Genogram helps psychiatry residents, nurses, psychologists, social workers and students draw a clear family genogram in minutes.

Add the family, mark how people relate, and the genogram draws itself with standard symbols and a key that lists only what your chart uses. Save it as a picture or a one-page A4 PDF for your case write-up or presentation.

THREE SIMPLE STEPS
• Family: start with the patient (index person), then add relatives with one tap: father, mother, brothers, sisters, partners, children, grandparents, great-grandparents, aunts, uncles, cousins and more.
• Relationships: draw lines between any two people: close, very close or enmeshed, distant, conflictual, cut off, hostile, abuse, neglect, caregiver.
• Genogram: the chart is laid out for you, siblings oldest to youngest, with a key underneath.

WHAT IT CAN SHOW
• Six generations and up to four partners for one person
• 25 kinds of couple relationship: married, separated, divorced, widowed, engaged, living together, dating and more
• Deceased (with age at death), twins, adopted and foster children, pregnancy, miscarriage, abortion and stillbirth
• Psychiatric disorder, substance use disorder and medical illness marks
• Years of birth and death, gender identity, pets and household members
• An outline around everyone who lives with the patient
• Notes under the chart

EASY TO USE
• Five made-up sample families to learn from
• Tap a person on the chart to edit them; pinch to zoom
• Move people sideways by hand when you want a different layout
• Undo while editing
• "Initials only" shortens every name on the chart
• Symbols and lines page and a How to use guide
• Light and dark themes, larger text, and English or Tagalog menus and forms

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking
• Works without the internet
• Your genograms are saved only on your phone or tablet
• Optional passcode lock with Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint
• Back up to a file and restore on another device
• Share one genogram as a file with a colleague

Genogram is a drawing tool for clinical and teaching work. It does not diagnose or give medical advice. Use initials or an alias, and follow your institution's rules on patient information.

Made by Isabella Navarro, MD, a psychiatry resident in the Philippines.

## Apple App Review notes [4000]

No sign-in is needed and the app makes no network requests. To see a full chart quickly, tap "Open a sample family" on the home screen (all sample families are invented). The optional passcode lock is under About; once a passcode is set, the app offers Face ID to unlock.
