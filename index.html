Genogram

Draw the family, see the pattern.

Add the family, mark how people relate, and the genogram draws itself with standard symbols and a key that matches the chart. Save it as a picture or a one-page PDF. Private: everything stays on your device.

You have genograms saved in this browser

Genogram is now an app for iPhone, iPad and Android, and this website no longer opens them. Download them as a backup file to keep them. In the app, scroll to the bottom of the home page, tap Import backup under “Back up your data”, and choose the file.

What it does

  • Three simple steps: add the family, mark relationships, and the chart is laid out for you.
  • Six generations, up to four partners, 25 kinds of couple relationship, twins, adoption, pregnancy and loss.
  • Psychiatric disorder, substance use and medical illness marks, and an outline around the household.
  • Save as a picture or a one-page A4 PDF for case write-ups and presentations.
  • No account, no server, no ads. Face ID or passcode lock.
